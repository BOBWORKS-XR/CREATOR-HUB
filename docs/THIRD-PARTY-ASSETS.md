# Third-Party Brand Assets

`src/sidequest-mark-white.svg` is the unmodified white SideQuest mark downloaded
from SideQuest's official branding page on 2026-09-10:

- https://sidequestvr.com/branding
- https://sidequestvr.com/assets/images/branding/Mark%20-%20White.svg

SideQuest's logo remains its property; the application's MIT license does not
relicense it. Preserve its colors, proportions and clear space. The black tile
and separate C badge identify the future Creator Converter; no public release
URL or installation capability is implied before SideQuest publishes one.

Selected Lucide controls retain `src/icons/LICENSE-lucide`.

## Creator Plugins mark

`src/icons/creator-plugins.png` is the user-approved Creator Plugins artwork:
three interlocking cyan/red puzzle faces form a removable corner of the gray Hub
cube. It was generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-09-15 and downscaled
to a transparent 256px PNG without redrawing the approved design. It is not a
Lucide or Unity logo. See `PLUGINS-ICON.md` for provenance and the generation prompt.
